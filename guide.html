How the Crowdsourced Blacklist Shields Everyone

Synchronized Peer Registry

Step 2 • Active Interception

If another person scans or inputs that exact URL or QR on LinkLocker AI, the client-side heuristic engine immediately intercepts execution and marks it with a 98% Risk Factor.

Step 3 • Threat Diagnostics

The interface renders the decoded data safely, highlights the submitted category, and provides a clear breakdown explaining why the asset is dangerous.
